# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences with equal weight:

- **Recruiters and hiring companies** evaluating Javier Cornejo as a full stack hire. Their job: judge fit quickly and decide whether to reach out for an interview.
- **Prospective freelance clients** looking for someone to build a product end to end. Their job: understand what Javier can deliver and start a conversation about a project.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Javier Cornejo, a full stack developer. It exists to turn a visit into contact: a job interview or a freelance inquiry. Success means a qualified visitor reaches out through one of the contact channels.

## Positioning

Full stack: Javier delivers across frontend and backend, end to end. The specific differentiator beyond "full stack" is not yet stated by the user and must not be invented.

## Operating Context

- Recruiters often skim between many candidates and may arrive from LinkedIn or a job application.
- Freelance clients arrive wanting proof of shipped work and a clear way to start a project.
- Content is published in both English and Spanish.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Astro, React and Tailwind CSS; deployed on Vercel (PRE from `develop`, PRO from `main`).
- Bilingual: English and Spanish, with a language switch (i18n).
- Contact channels: email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and an on-site contact form. The form needs a delivery backend or service; which one is undecided.
- Current state: a single "Incoming" coming-soon page over an animated Silk background (`src/components/Silk.tsx`) with Instagram, LinkedIn and GitHub links in the footer, plus a 404 page.
- Live URL: https://javiercornejo.vercel.app

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Javier Cornejo.

## Evidence on Hand

The user confirmed they have real material for:

- Projects (personal and/or professional, with links).
- Work experience (companies, roles, dates).
- Content (articles, talks, videos, or posts).

None of it is in the repository yet; it must be supplied by the user. No testimonials exist: do not fabricate testimonials, client logos, metrics, or claims.

## Product Principles

1. Every page earns contact: the path to email, form, LinkedIn, or GitHub is always one step away.
2. Serve both audiences without splitting the site: recruiters get fast proof of fit, clients get proof of delivery.
3. Show real work only; an empty section beats an invented one.
4. English and Spanish are equal citizens, not a translation afterthought.
